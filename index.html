Our product

AI CFO

AI CFO keeps a business's books for it. Owners type or say what they sold or spent, snap a receipt or upload a bank statement, and AI CFO records it, works out their profit and answers questions about their money. It works on the web and on WhatsApp, in any country and currency.

  • Profit, income and spending for any week, month or year
  • Weekly, monthly and yearly reports, checked by our team before they are sent
  • PDF statements and Excel files for bank loan and grant applications
Visit aicfo.jegtech.com.ng

How businesses use it

"Sold 5 crates of drinks 32k." AI CFO records the sale, adds it to this week's figures and keeps a clean record the owner can download at any time. The totals are calculated by the system from the owner's own records, never guessed.

What we stand for

Accurate numbers

Every figure comes from the records the business owner confirms. We show our working and never invent data.

Human review

Reports are reviewed by our team before they reach a customer.

Your data stays yours

We never sell customer data. Owners can download their records or delete their account at any time.
